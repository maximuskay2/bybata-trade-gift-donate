Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

ByBata Inc. (“ByBata”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the ByBata mobile app, website at bybata.com, and related services (the “Service”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

ByBata Inc.
Lagos, Nigeria
Email: support@bybata.com
Website: https://bybata.com

For users in regions with dedicated privacy representatives, we will publish additional contact details on this page when applicable.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account data — name, email address, phone number, password (stored in hashed form), profile photo, bio, and optional location text you choose to display.
  • Verification (KYC) — government-issued identification and related verification data when you request identity verification or certain wallet features.
  • Listings & content — photos, titles, descriptions, and metadata for trades, gifts, donation campaigns, ads, and messages you post.
  • Transactions — trade offers, gift claims, donations, wallet transfers, withdrawals, and promotion purchases.
  • Communications — messages with other users, support requests, and feedback you send us.
  • Preferences — privacy toggles (e.g. public profile, show email/phone/location), notification settings, language, currency, and timezone.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & app data — device type, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers, and crash/diagnostic logs.
  • Usage data — features used, pages viewed, search queries, interactions with listings, and referral attribution.
  • Network data — IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and timestamps of activity.
  • Push tokens — if you enable push notifications, we store a device token to deliver alerts.

2.3 Information from others

  • Other users may share content that references you (e.g. trade messages, reviews).
  • Payment processors may provide transaction status (not full card numbers stored by us where avoided).
  • Identity verification partners may return verification results.

3. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Operate trades, gifts, campaigns, wallet (BYC), Karma, referrals, and advertising features.
  • Process payments, withdrawals, and fraud prevention checks.
  • Verify identity where required for safety or compliance.
  • Send service-related messages (security alerts, transaction updates, policy changes).
  • Send push and email notifications according to your settings (including optional marketing emails if you opt in).
  • Improve, secure, and debug the Service (including anti-abuse and duplicate-image detection).
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and protect users and the public.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, product improvement, and community safety, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — e.g. optional marketing emails, certain location features, or push notifications where required.
  • Legal obligation — tax, financial, or law-enforcement requirements.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

RecipientPurpose
Other usersAs you direct via profile visibility settings, listings, messages, and public campaign pages.
Service providersHosting, cloud storage, email delivery, push notifications, analytics, payment processing, and identity verification — under contractual confidentiality obligations.
Legal & safetyWhen required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the Service.
Business transfersIn connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice where required by law.

Verification documents are used only for identity and fraud prevention and are not sold or shared for third-party marketing.

6. Your privacy choices in the app

You can control several settings under Settings → Privacy:

  • Whether your profile is public.
  • Whether to show your email, phone number, or location on your profile.
  • Whether other users can send you direct messages.

Under Settings → Notifications you can manage push, email, and marketing preferences.

You may block users and manage active sessions from Settings where available.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain records for legal, fraud-prevention, or backup purposes.

You may request a copy of your data via Settings → Security & data → Download my data where available.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS), access controls, and secure handling of verification materials. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; you use the Service at your own risk and should protect your credentials.

9. International transfers

Your data may be processed in Nigeria and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact support@bybata.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format where technically feasible.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, email support@bybata.com from your registered email address. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

12. Cookies and similar technologies (website)

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies for security and basic functionality. We may use analytics on the marketing site to understand traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings where applicable.

13. Third-party links

The Service may link to third-party sites or services (e.g. app stores, payment pages). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes may be communicated in the app or by email. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.
