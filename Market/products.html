Executive Summary

BYBATA is a community-powered sharing economy mobile platform where people trade without cash, give gifts freely, and crowdfund personal and business needs — all while building KARMA (trust reputation) and using BYC (bYbata Coin) for in-app value. The emotional core: “You Are Not Alone.”

🎯 Elevator pitch (for campaigns):
“BYBATA is where neighbors trade without cash, gift freely, and fund each other’s dreams — while earning KARMA you can trust. Browse locally, chat to negotiate, and grow together.”

The Three Pillars

TRADE

Swap items

No cash. Negotiate locally. Earn +50 KARMA per completed trade.

GIFT

Give for free

Pass it forward. Earn +30 KARMA. Gift chains & surprise gifts.

DONATE

Crowdfund campaigns

Reward tiers. Community support. Earn +40 KARMA per donation.

KARMA + BYC → Trust & in‑app currency that fuels the ecosystem.

💰 Wallet & BYC (bYbata Coin)

In-app currency for funding, transfers, promotions, withdrawals. Fund via Paystack, Stripe, PayPal, Flutterwave. Users can boost listings, buy funding packages, and send BYC via QR.

⭐ KARMA trust tiers

Newcomer → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Legend. Leaderboard, achievements, and earn guide.

The New Home Experience (Home V2) – Key Differentiator

  • Guest home: purple gradient, command pill “Trade, Donate or Gift”. Browse listings, no account required.
  • Member home: personalized “Will you Trade, Donate or Gift, {firstName}?” + bottom navigation.
  • Conversational intent: type “swap”, “gift”, “campaign” → app routes to correct hub.
